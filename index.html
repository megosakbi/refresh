Where do I add Google Drive links?+

Open script.js and replace each tool's download value.

Can I add more extensions?+

Yes. Duplicate an object inside the TOOLS array and edit it.

Can I replace the avatar and tool images?+

Yes. Replace the SVG files inside the assets folder with your own images and update their paths in script.js.
